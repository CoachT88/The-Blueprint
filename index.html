The part nobody explains

Why one muscle causes all of it

An erection is blood pressure. Blood flows into your penis, and something has to stop it from flowing straight back out. That something is your pelvic floor. It squeezes the base and traps the blood in.

A weak pelvic floor cannot hold the seal. You get hard and then you slowly lose it, and the harder you concentrate the faster it goes.

A too tight pelvic floor is the one almost nobody gets right. A muscle held clenched all day is not strong, it is stuck. It cannot squeeze any harder when you need it to, it chokes the blood flow going in, and it puts you on a hair trigger. It looks exactly like weakness and the usual advice makes it worse.

Women get taught this after childbirth. Men get taught nothing, then get sold a pill that props up the symptom for four hours and does nothing to the muscle.

Section 07 · Straight talk

What this is not.

It is not a pill and it is not instant

This is muscle training and it works the way training works. A few sessions a week, for several weeks, before the change is obvious. If you want something that works tonight, do not buy this.

It is not a diagnosis

Pain, sudden changes, or symptoms that came on fast need a doctor. The app tells you that instead of training you through it. This is built for the slow decline, not for an alarm.

More is not better here

Rest days are in the schedule because this tissue needs them. Doing extra is the single most common way men make themselves worse instead of better.

It is not a promise

Bodies are different and anybody guaranteeing you a specific result is selling you something. What you get here is the right work, in the right order, with somebody to ask when you are stuck.
