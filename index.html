Section 01 · The part you don’t say out loud

You noticed. You just haven’t said it to anyone.

Maybe it takes longer than it used to. Maybe it goes soft halfway and you have to work to get it back. Maybe you finish before you want to and you have started planning around it — the drink first, the excuse ready, the night you quietly avoid.

Maybe nothing is wrong exactly. It is just not what it was at twenty-two, and you have started wondering whether that is simply the rest of your life now.

You have not told your doctor, because the appointment would take three weeks and four minutes and end with a pamphlet. You have not told your partner, because saying it out loud makes it real. So you searched at midnight, found forums full of men guessing, and closed the tab.

Memo

What you were never told

The pelvic floor is a sheet of muscle that sits at the base of your core. It is involved in getting hard, staying hard, and finishing when you choose to.

Like any muscle, it can be weak — or it can be too tight, which is the part almost nobody gets right. A tight pelvic floor causes many of the same problems as a weak one, and the usual advice makes it worse.

Women get told about this after childbirth. Men get told nothing at all, then get sold a pill that treats the symptom for four hours.

Section 02 · What this actually is

Training, not a pill.

A pill gives you four hours. Training gives you back the thing itself. The difference is that training takes weeks and nobody sells it to you, because there is no bottle to ship.

01

It knows which problem you have

Too weak and too tight need opposite work. Get that backwards and you make it worse. The programme separates them instead of handing every man the same squeeze.

02

It runs the session for you

You do not read a plan and try to remember it. The app counts, times the breathing, tells you when to hold and when to release, and ends the session when it should end.

03

It gets harder when you do

Four levels. You do not pick one because it sounds impressive — the next one opens when your own logged sessions say you are ready for it.

Section 07 · Straight talk

What this is not.

It is not a pill and not instant

This is muscle training. It works the way training works: a few sessions a week, for weeks, before the change is obvious. If you want something that works tonight, this is the wrong purchase.

It is not a diagnosis

Sudden changes, pain, or symptoms that came on fast deserve a doctor, and the app will tell you so rather than train you through it. Training is for the slow decline, not the alarm.

It is not more is better

Rest days are scheduled because this tissue needs them. Doing extra is the most common way men get worse instead of better.

It is not a promise

Bodies differ, and anyone guaranteeing you a specific outcome is selling something. What is offered here is the right work, ordered properly, with someone to ask.
