An erection that works sometimes can still feel unreliable.

What is actually in the app. A weekly erection quality routine of three short sessions, on Sunday, Wednesday and Friday. Each one is easy breathing at a slow pace, a pelvic floor release exercise, and one gentle mobility movement that changes through the week: Cat-Cow, deep squat and Happy Baby. Monday and Thursday are a lighter supporting practice, and Tuesday and Saturday are full rest with nothing asked of you. The sessions stay the same from week to week.

Rate your erection quality from 1 to 10 at the end of any session, optionally. The app charts those ratings over time, the week view shows which sessions you have completed, and the weekly report gives that week's average and whether the ratings moved up or down across it, once you have rated at least two sessions in the week. A short symptom check at setup helps the app decide which pelvic floor exercises to show you. That check is a precaution built into the app, not a diagnosis. If you tell the app something hurt, it pauses the pelvic floor release and keeps the breathing and the movement.

The honest limit. This is practice for easy breathing, awareness of pelvic tension and comfortable movement. It does not treat erectile dysfunction, and no one can promise what it will do for your erections. Your own ratings show your pattern over time. Persistent pain, urinary symptoms or erection changes need professional evaluation.
