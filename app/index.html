How to Measure

BPEL — Bone Pressed Erect Length

  1. 1.Achieve a full erection (EQ 9–10).
  2. 2.Place ruler flat on top, pressing firmly against the pubic bone.
  3. 3.Measure from pubic bone to the tip. Record to nearest 0.25″.

MSEG — Mid-Shaft Erect Girth

  1. 1.Achieve a full erection (EQ 9–10).
  2. 2.Wrap a soft tape measure (or string) around the mid-shaft.
  3. 3.Measure snugly — not tight. Record to nearest 0.25″.

Consistency is key

Measure at the same time of day, same EQ level, same position. Small daily fluctuations are normal — trends over weeks are what matter.

    User Manual

    Everything you need to use this app correctly

    Your home screen

    Where the app opens, and what is on it

    This is where the app opens every time. Here is what everything on it means:

    Week Progress

    Counts the training sessions you have completed this week against the ones your schedule asked for. A prescribed rest day is not a gap, so taking it costs you nothing. This replaced the old day streak, which broke every time you rested exactly as planned.

    XP & Rank

    XP is earned every session. The progress bar shows how close you are to the next rank. Ranks go: Recruit → Operative → Specialist → Expert → Master → Legend. Higher difficulty earns more XP per session.

    Your Week

    The seven days under your Today card. It shows what is planned, what is complete, and when you are resting. Each day is color-coded: blue for Length, green for Girth, purple for Stamina, grey for Rest. Today is highlighted, and a day you finished carries a tick.

    Daily Check-In

    Log your sleep quality and muscle soreness before every session. If you report moderate or high soreness, the app will recommend Recovery Protocol instead of training. Take that recommendation. Hard training on top of real soreness is not where progress comes from.

    Hydration Tracker

    Log your water intake throughout the day using the oz or L toggle. Tap the 1L, 2L, 3L milestone bubbles when you hit each one, or enter a custom amount. The minimum target is 3L daily. The app adjusts its hydration warning based on your difficulty level and time of day — a morning warning is encouragement, an evening warning at Advanced tier is serious.

    Personal Records

    Shows your all-time bests: highest single-session XP, and highest week XP. These update automatically when you break a record.

    Training Sessions

    Length, Girth, and how they work

    Tap Begin Session to start. After the pre-flight check you pick your mission. Length and Girth then run the warmup phase first, because cold tissue does not stretch. Stamina and Recovery start straight away.

    Length Protocol

    Targets the suspensory ligament and tunica through directional tension. The session consists of one exercise: Directional Pulls.

    Directional Pulls — 5 directions (Out, Up, Down, Left, Right) performed in sequence. Each direction is a separate timed set. The button always shows which direction you are working before you start. EQ target: 3–4 (flaccid to very soft).

    Girth Protocol

    1. WET JELQ

    Apply lubrication. Form an 'OK' grip at the base. Make firm, upward dynamic strokes along the shaft, stopping just before the glans (head). Keep grip at 60–80% EQ to maximize vascular engorgement. Apply even pressure.

    2. THE ULI

    Achieve maximum engorgement (80% EQ). Apply a firm 'OK' clamp at the very base, trapping blood in the shaft. Hold statically. Maintain hydraulic pressure for tissue expansion.

    3. ACTIVE REST (Box Breathing + Reverse Kegels)

    Rest your grip and relax the tissue. For 30–60 seconds at the end of each round, perform Box Breathing (Inhale 4s, Hold 4s, Exhale 4s, Hold 4s) while actively performing Reverse Kegels (gently pushing the pelvic floor down/out). This normalizes blood flow and prevents pelvic floor tension. Re-lubricate before the next round.

    Beginner: 3 Rounds · Mid: 4 Rounds · Advanced: 5 Rounds · Elite: 5 Rounds (longer durations)

    Stamina & Blood Flow

    A separate protocol focused on arousal management and vascular conditioning. Runs as standard sets — not a circuit.

    Edging — Controlled Hold — Slow strokes to the arousal plateau, then hold. Cycle controlled arousal for the full duration. EQ: 70–80%. Trains stamina through disciplined repetition.

    Lateral Compression — Gentle coronal ridge squeeze using thumb and two fingers just below the head. 10–20 second holds. EQ: 60–70%.

    The most important number in this protocol

    EQ is your Erection Quality on a scale of 1 to 10. It tells you how much blood volume is in the tissue and therefore what kind of stress it can handle.

    Why does EQ matter so much?

    Too soft during girth work means there is not enough internal pressure to stress the tissue — you are wasting the set. Too hard means the tissue is fully pressurized with no room to expand — you risk microtears and overstress. The target EQ for each exercise is the sweet spot where pressure is high enough to create adaptation but not so high that you risk injury.

    EQ Check-In After Sessions

    After every session the app asks you to rate your EQ during training. This data feeds your weekly report so you can track whether your EQ is trending up or down over time. Low EQ trends often correlate with poor sleep, low hydration, or overtraining.

    EQ and the Elite Tier

    Unlocking Elite requires a sustained average EQ of 9+ across your last 10 sessions. This requirement exists because Elite volume is only safe when vascular function is at its peak. If your EQ is not consistently high, the load will outpace your capacity.

    Difficulty Tiers

    Beginner through Elite — how locking works

    The app has four difficulty tiers. They control the duration and number of sets for every exercise. Higher tiers earn more XP per session.

    Beginner

    Shorter durations, fewer sets. Always available, to anyone, at any time. Dropping to it never needs permission and never costs you anything you have earned.

    Intermediate

    The default, and the standard working tier. Always available. This is where the majority of your training will happen.

    Advanced

    Earned, not waited out. It opens once you have put together four solid training weeks out of your last eight, and it stays open once you have it. If your recent sessions have been very hard going, or there is not enough history to judge yet, it stays shut for now and the app tells you which. Time served at Intermediate counts for nothing on its own, because sitting at a tier is not training.

    Elite

    Three things at once: you have reached Advanced, you have the same four solid weeks behind you, and your average EQ across your recent sessions is 9 or above. Fewer than three rated sessions is not enough to judge, and it is not treated as a pass. Tapping the locked Elite button tells you which part is missing.

    Deload Weeks

    Once you have put together four solid training weeks, the week that follows is a deload week. Sets drop by one and durations come down, from that week's first session to its last, and the app flags it with an orange banner. It is one week only. Whatever you manage inside it, the next week goes back to normal and the count starts again. This is how the programme is planned rather than a verdict on how your week went, and the session count stays the same, so a deload week can still be a full week of training.

    Recovery Protocol

    8 exercises — the other half of the protocol

    Recovery is not a rest from the protocol — it is the other half of it. Gains happen during recovery. The 8 exercises are split into two categories.

    Pelvic Floor Work (4 exercises)

    Kegel Contractions — Trains voluntary contraction. Pelvic floor muscle training has reasonable evidence behind it for erectile function and ejaculatory control, but only if your floor is not already too tight. A hypertonic floor gets worse with kegels, not better. Take the Pelvic Floor Check in this Manual first; if it flags you, the app removes these from your protocol automatically. 3 sets × 60s. 3 seconds on, 3 seconds full release.

    Reverse Kegel Stretch — The opposite of the kegel. Trains the floor to let go rather than squeeze. 3 sets × 45s. Breathe into your lower belly and let the muscles between your penis and anus open and drop, like the feeling of letting gas out. No pushing, no straining.

    Pelvic Floor Release — Deep passive decompression using breath and gravity. No active movement. 3 sets × 45s.

    Pelvic Floor Endurance Hold — 40% contraction sustained for the full set. Builds motor control and stamina, not peak strength. 3 sets × 60s.

    The Big Four (4 exercises)

    Deep Squat — Malasana — A deep hip and groin stretch for the muscles around the pelvis. Feet flat, elbows nudging knees wide. 2 sets × 60s.

    Happy Baby — A quick way to take tension out of the hips and pelvic floor. On your back, feet grabbed, knees toward armpits. Rock side to side. 2 sets × 45s.

    Pigeon Pose — Targets the glutes and deep hip rotators that pull on the pelvis from sitting. One leg folded in front, other stretched back. 2 sets × 60s (switch sides each set).

    Butterfly Stretch — Releases the adductors (inner thighs), which sit close to the pelvic floor and commonly get tight from sitting. Soles together, knees out, hinge forward at the hips. 2 sets × 45s.

    Daily Recommendations

    The app recommends different exercises based on time of day. Morning: open the hips (Squat + Happy Baby). Afternoon: counteract sitting (Pigeon + Butterfly). Night: pelvic floor protocol (all 4 floor exercises). You can always customize the selection or tap Select All 8.

    Rest days

    On scheduled rest days, Length and Girth are not available and Recovery Protocol is. This is intentional. Rest is part of the programme, not a gap in it, and the tissue needs mechanical rest between sessions.

    Safety & Warning Signs

    When to stop — no exceptions

    Stop immediately if you experience:

    • Sharp, electric, or stinging pain during any exercise
    • Tingling or numbness anywhere in the tissue
    • Visible discoloration (dark purple or bruising)
    • Glans (head) engorgement or pressure during Uli or Squeeze
    • Coldness or loss of sensation at the glans
    • Any pain that persists more than 24 hours after a session

    Normal sensations vs warning signs

    A mild dull ache at the base after length work is normal and usually means the ligament was loaded. Skin-level soreness from jelqing without enough lube is a technique error, not an injury, but stop and reapply. Mild fatigue in the pelvic floor after kegel work can be normal. Aching, pressure, or a heavy feeling in the perineum is not — that is a sign to stop contracting and switch to release work, and to take the Pelvic Floor Check in this Manual. Any sharp, electric, or stinging sensation is a hard stop — no exceptions.

    The soreness check-in

    Log your muscle soreness daily on your home screen. The app will recommend Recovery Protocol if you report moderate or high soreness. Take that recommendation. Training hard on top of real soreness does not speed anything up, and backing off for a day costs you very little.

    The soreness scale is asking about muscle soreness, the dull ache that follows work. It is not asking about sharp pain, electric or stinging sensations, unusual joint pain or anything nerve-like. Those are not a volume problem. Stop the exercise, and get them looked at by a professional if they persist or recur.

    Tips for Best Results

    What separates guys who gain from guys who plateau

    Never skip the warmup

    The 10-minute warmup phase is not optional before Length or Girth work. Connective tissue is more compliant warm than cold, which is why every training discipline warms up before it loads anything. Use a warm towel, warm shower, or the warmup timer in the app. Stamina and Recovery sessions do not need it.

    Hydration is structural

    Water is a major component of connective tissue, and turning up well hydrated is part of turning up in good shape. Spread your intake across the day in sips rather than drinking a lot right before training. 3L is the daily target this app tracks, not a line you cross into danger.

    Sleep is when you grow

    Growth hormone peaks during deep sleep. The tissue you stressed during training remodels and expands during sleep. 7–9 hours is part of the protocol, not a bonus.

    Consistency over intensity

    One solid session per week for 6 months beats aggressive daily training for 2 weeks then quitting. The tissue needs repeated, consistent stimulation with adequate recovery between sessions.

    Do the Recovery Protocol

    The Big Four and pelvic floor work on rest days are part of the programme, not optional extras. Mobility in the surrounding hips and adductors can support comfortable pelvic movement and relaxation, especially for people who spend long periods sitting.

    Trust the tier locks

    The 4-week locks on Advanced and Elite are not obstacles — they are the protocol. Your tissue adapts in layers. Jumping tiers before the minimum time causes injury, not faster gains.

    Where your data lives

    Everything is stored in this app's own private database and nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any advertising or analytics company. There are no third-party trackers in this app.
