Erection quality · Note 01Sleep is information, not a verdict

The thought: One difficult night can make you question your ability.

What to understand: Sleep affects energy, attention and sexual response, but one night cannot diagnose a problem.

Try this: For seven mornings, jot down your sleep duration and how rested you feel. Look for patterns, not a perfect score.

Remember: Patterns beat a single bad night.
