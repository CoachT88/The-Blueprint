Purchase and access

How much does The Blueprint cost?

The current price is $99 as a one-time payment. The homepage lists the features included with access.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. The current offer is a one-time payment.

How do I sign in after purchasing?

Open the app sign-in page and use the same email address you paid with. For a purchase on this site that is the address on your Stripe receipt; if you bought through Ko-fi before October 2026, it is the one you used there, and your access carries over unchanged. If you need help, contact support with your purchase email and a brief description of the issue. Do not send your password.

What if I forgot my password?

Use the password-reset option on the app sign-in screen. Open the reset email and follow its instructions. Check your spam folder if the message does not arrive.

Training and results

What can I work on?

The app includes guided sessions related to stamina, pelvic floor awareness, recovery, erection quality habits, and optional manual length and girth routines.

Will I gain length or girth?

Men in long-running enhancement communities report changes from manual stretching, jelqing and other methods. Some describe more than an inch, often after combining techniques over time. The Blueprint includes optional guided manual routines, measurements and recovery tools if you want to explore that goal. Individual outcomes vary, and no specific gain is guaranteed. See the original progress reports.

Can the app treat erectile dysfunction or premature ejaculation?

The app is an educational training tool. It does not diagnose or treat either condition. A qualified healthcare professional can evaluate persistent erection or ejaculation concerns.

How quickly will I see results?

Timing varies by person, training goal, and underlying health factors. Some changes may not occur. Use your logs to review your experience and prioritize safety.

Can I change my goal?

The app provides a goal-change option after initial setup. Review your current program before changing direction.

Safety and privacy

When should I stop a session?

Stop if you notice pain, bruising, numbness, bleeding, discoloration, or any change in function. Seek medical advice, especially when symptoms continue or appear suddenly.

Is The Blueprint for adults only?

Yes. You must be at least 18 years old.
